Yang Zhao




  

   M.S., NJUHML Group
   School of Atmospheric Sciences, Nanjing University

   Email: zhaoy2024@smail.nju.edu.cn



Yang Zhao is an M.S. student at the School of Atmospheric Sciences, Nanjing University. He completed his undergraduate study at Nanjing University and continued into graduate study in 2024.

Research Interests

Deep-learning-based spatiotemporal downscaling, AI weather foundation models, typhoon track forecasting, and intensity correction.

Awards

  • First Prize, 19th Challenge Cup Special Competition “Open Competition for Top Challenges” (2024)
  • Meritorious Winner, MCM/ICM (2022)

Selected Work

  • Poster presentation on deep-learning-based spatiotemporal downscaling at the 3rd China Digital Earth Conference
  • Co-author of a university-level outstanding thesis team project on interpretability in deep-learning downscaling
  • First inventor of a granted patent on UNet-based PPI radar echo filtering

Supervisor

Professor Huiling Yuan
