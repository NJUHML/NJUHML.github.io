## About Me

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree at the Nanjing-Helsinki Institute in Atmospheric and Earth System Sciences, Nanjing University, supervised by [Professor Sandro M. Ferreira Veiga][Sandro] and Professor [Yann Salmon](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/about-us/people/people-finder/yann-salmon-9363739). My main research direction is extreme climate events.

Before joining Nanjing University, I received my B.S. degree in Water Conservancy Engineering from the College of Hydrology and Water Resources, Hohai University in 2023, and then received my M.S. degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2026. During my M.S. studies, I mainly worked on deep learning methods for remote sensing image recognition and processing.
